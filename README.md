# Yutori 🍜

### A study tracker that makes progress visible.

Yutori started with a simple problem I’ve experienced as a student: **staying consistent with studying is hard.**

You can spend hours studying and still feel like you have nothing to show for it. I wanted to make that effort feel a little more rewarding.

**So I made Yutori.**

## The idea

Yutori combines a study timer, statistics, and a visual representation of your progress.

The main visual concept comes from **Japanese food bowls**. As you study, your bowl slowly evolves, turning your effort into something visible.

The name **Yutori (ゆとり)** can refer to having extra space, time, or mental room. I chose it because I wanted Yutori to feel calm and encouraging, rather than rushed or stressful.

## What it does

* Track study sessions
* See your statistics and history
* Watch your bowl evolve over time
* Manage premium features with RevenueCat
